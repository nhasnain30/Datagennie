About the Webinar

Algae and seaweed are emerging as promising next-generation feedstocks for sustainable biofuels due to their rapid growth, high biomass productivity, ability to thrive on non-arable land and in marine environments, and potential to decarbonize hard-to-abate sectors such as aviation and shipping.

Although commercial deployment is still evolving, significant advances have been made worldwide in cultivation systems, integrated biorefineries, conversion technologies, and demonstration projects. Bringing together leading experts from research, industry, and policy, this webinar will explore the latest technological developments, commercialization experiences, and global best practices to accelerate the sustainable deployment of algae- and seaweed-based biofuels.
