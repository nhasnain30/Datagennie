Privileged Access Management (PAM)

Secure Your Most Critical Accounts. Control Privileged Access with Confidence.

Privileged accounts provide access to your organization’s most sensitive systems, applications, databases, and network infrastructure. Without proper controls, these accounts can become a significant cybersecurity risk.

DataGennie delivers WhiteSwan Privileged Access Management (PAM) solutions to help organizations secure privileged identities, enforce least-privilege access, improve operational visibility, and strengthen compliance with industry security standards.

As an Authorized Distributor of WhiteSwan PAM in India, DataGennie provides licensing, solution consulting, deployment assistance, and ongoing technical support to help enterprises implement a secure privileged access strategy.

Key capabilities include:

  • Privileged Account Discovery
  • Secure Credential Vault
  • Password Rotation & Lifecycle Management
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
  • Just-in-Time (JIT) Privileged Access*
  • Session Monitoring & Recording*
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Integration*
  • Centralized Access Policies
  • Audit Logs & Compliance Reporting
  • Enterprise Directory Integration
  • Secure Remote Administrative Access*
  • High Availability Architecture*

Why Privileged Access Management Matters

Privileged credentials are among the primary targets for cyber attackers. A modern PAM solution helps organizations

  • Protect privileged accounts from unauthorized access
  • Control administrator access across critical systems
  • Reduce insider threats
  • Improve visibility into privileged activities
  • Support regulatory and compliance requirements
  • Strengthen enterprise cybersecurity posture
